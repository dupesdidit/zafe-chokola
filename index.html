Our Story

A sweet dream, born from joy.

Zafe Chokola began with a moment of self-reflection — a long, honest look at the things that bring me the most joy. Chocolate was right at the top of the list! So I signed up for an online chocolate course, and the rest is history.

The name is an ode to my Caribbean roots on the island of St. Lucia. In St. Lucian Kwéyòl, zafè means “business” and chokola means “chocolate” — so Zafe Chokola is, quite simply, a chocolate business, with the words the other way around.

Every piece is made in the hope of sparking a joyful memory — the kind you’ll still be smiling about long after the last bite.

— Founder & Chocolatier

Shipping

Markets & events

Prefer to pick out your treats in person? You’ll find us at farmers’ markets and events. Follow @zafechokola2025 to see where we’ll be next.

Paying

We accept credit and debit cards (secure online card checkout is coming soon) and Cash App.

Freshness & storage

Our chocolates stay fresh for up to 6 months. Store them somewhere cool and dry, away from direct sunlight, heat and strong smells.

Damaged or melted?

If your order arrives damaged or melted, we’ll refund your payment. Just email us at Zafechokola@gmail.com.

Allergen notice

Our products and kitchen contain tree nuts, peanuts, dairy, gluten/wheat and eggs. If you have a food allergy, please get in touch before ordering.
