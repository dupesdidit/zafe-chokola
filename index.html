Our Story

A small atelier with a patient philosophy.

Zafe Chokola began at a kitchen table with a marble slab, a thermometer and an obsession with getting the snap just right. Today we still temper every batch by hand, sourcing cacao from growers we know by name.

Each season our chocolatiers develop a handful of new flavours — inspired by markets, travels and the spice drawer — and paint every shell before it is filled, sealed and boxed.
